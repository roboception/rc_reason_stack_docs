Safety
======

.. Warning:: The operator must have read and understood all of the instructions in this
   manual before handling the |rc_xxx| product.

.. only:: rc_cube or rc_reason_stack

    .. Warning:: If operating the |rc_xxx| with |rc_visard| product(s),
        the operator must have read and understood all of the safety, installation,
        and maintenance instructions given in the |rc_visard| manual.

.. Note:: The term "operator" refers to anyone responsible for any of the following tasks
   performed in conjunction with |rc_xxx|:

    * Installation
    * Maintenance
    * Inspection
    * Calibration
    * Programming
    * Decommissioning

This manual explains the |rc_xxx|'s various components and general operations regarding the
product's whole life-cycle, from installation through operation to decommissioning.

The drawings and photos in this documentation are representative examples; differences may exist
between them and the delivered product.

General warnings
----------------

.. Note:: Any use of the |rc_xxx| in noncompliance with these warnings is inappropriate and
   may cause injury or damage as well as void the warranty.

.. only:: rc_visard or rc_visard_ng

    .. Warning::
        * The |rc_xxx| needs to be properly mounted before use.
        * All cable sets need to be secured to the |rc_xxx| and the mount.
        * Cords must be at most 30 m long.
        * An appropriate DC power source must supply power to the |rc_xxx|.
        * Each |rc_xxx| must be connected to a separate power supply.
        * The |rc_xxx|'s housing must be grounded.
        * The |rc_xxx|'s and any related equipment's safety guidelines must always be satisfied.
        * The |rc_xxx| does not fall under the purview of the machinery, low voltage, or medical directives.

.. only:: rc_cube

    .. Warning::
        * The |rc_xxx|'s and any related equipment's safety guidelines must always be satisfied.
        * The |rc_xxx| does not fall under the purview of the machinery or medical directives.

.. only:: rc_visard or rc_visard_ng

    .. rubric:: Risk assessment and final application:

    The |rc_xxx| may be used on a robot. Robot, |rc_xxx|, and any other equipment used in the final
    application must be evaluated with a risk assessment. The system integrator's duty is to
    ensure respect for all local safety measures and regulations. Depending on the application,
    there may be risks that need additional protection/safety measures.

Intended use
------------

.. only:: rc_visard or rc_visard_ng

    The |rc_xxx| is an industrial 3D vision sensor with processing unit intended 
    for integration into automation and robotics applications. Its intended purpose is the acquisition and processing of 
    image data and the provision of results (e.g. 3D data, detected poses) to a connected robot controller or host system.

.. only:: rc_cube

    The |rc_cube| is an industrial processing unit intended to be used in combination with a 3D camera for data
    acquisition (e.g., stereo images), for integration into automation and robotics applications. 
    Its intended purpose is the acquisition and processing of 
    image data and the provision of results (e.g. 3D data, detected poses) to a connected robot controller or host system.

    .. Warning:: The |rc_cube| is only intended for stationary installation.

.. only:: rc_reason_stack

    The |rc_xxx| consists of docker containers for processing of industrial 3D vision data intended for 
    integration into automation and robotics applications. Their intended purpose is the acquisition 
    and processing of image data and the provision of results (e.g. 3D data, detected poses) 
    to a connected robot controller or host system.

.. only:: not rc_reason_stack

    The |rc_xxx| is designed and intended to be operated in a trusted environment with a direct, 
    point-to-point (1:1) connection between the device and the connected camera, robot controller, 
    or host system. In this configuration, the devices are not intended to be connected to, or made 
    accessible from, an open or untrusted network.

.. only:: rc_reason_stack

    These products are designed and intended to be operated in a trusted environment. Cameras should be 
    connected with a direct, point-to-point (1:1) connection between the device on which the containers 
    are executed and the connected cameras.

Several interfaces required for interoperability with robot controllers and industrial systems rely 
on established industry standards or vendor-specific protocols that do not support encryption or 
authentication. Enabling these interfaces outside a trusted, isolated network may expose the system 
to cybersecurity risks and is outside the intended use of the product.

The operator is responsible for ensuring that the operating environment matches these conditions, 
including physical access control to the device, network isolation, and secure configuration of 
the host system where applicable. Operating the product outside its intended use, in particular by 
exposing unsecured interfaces to an untrusted network, may compromise the security and reliability of 
the system and is not covered by the product's cybersecurity risk assessment.

.. Warning:: The |rc_xxx| is **NOT** intended for safety critical applications.

The |rc_xxx| may be used only within the scope of its technical specification. Any other use of the
product is deemed unintended use. |company| will not be liable for any damages resulting from any
improper or unintended use.

.. Warning:: Always comply with local and/or national laws, regulations and directives on automation
   safety and general machine safety.
